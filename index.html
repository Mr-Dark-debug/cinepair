⚡ PEER-TO-PEER SHARING

Talk while you watch

CinePair uses WebRTC to send calls and screen sharing between devices. A Python signaling service coordinates the connection, and a TURN relay may be needed on restrictive networks. Connection quality depends on each network.

⚙️ Product Highlights

Everything for a shared night in.

🍿

Dynamic Profile Overlays

Resize, reposition, and customize. When screen-sharing, overlay your partner's camera frame. Drag it anywhere, toggle circle or square frames, or hide your view instantly to optimize movie visual space.

💬

Reactions and chat

Send a message or emoji reaction in the room while the film plays. A passcode adds device-side encryption to chat.

🔒

Zero Registration

Guest rooms need no registration. Set a nickname, create a room code, and share it with your partner. Add a passcode to encrypt room chat on your devices.

🔧 Operational Mechanics

Cinema sessions in three simple steps.

Start as a guest with no account or use optional accounts and pairing codes to stay connected.

Download Tauri Client
1

Open a Room

Open CinePair, choose your screen name, set room limits or an approval lobby, and share the generated room code. Add a separate passcode if you want encrypted chat.

2

Invite Friends

Share the room code and, if you set one, the passcode. When the approval lobby is on, guests wait for the host to admit them.

3

Enjoy the Movie

Play a supported public video in sync, or share a screen when capture is allowed. Keep your cameras and chat close to the film.
