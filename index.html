⚡ LATENCY-FREE SHARING

Pure P2P Perfect Negotiation

By building strictly on top of WebRTC Peer-to-Peer channels and using W3C's Perfect Negotiation pattern, CinePair connects your devices directly. No video flows to our servers, keeping streaming bandwidth high and latency near-zero.

🔧 Operational Mechanics

Cinema sessions in three simple steps.

We've stripped away the overhead. No accounts, no setup wizards, no premium monthly subscriptions.

Download Tauri Client
1

Open a Room

Launch the CinePair native app, choose your custom screen name, set room limits or waiting lobbies, and generate your secure, random P2P passcode.

2

Invite Friends

Send your unique passcode to your partner or friends. When they enter the passcode, they stand in your approval lobby queue until you accept them.

3

Enjoy the Movie

Start sharing your high-fidelity browser stream or local player. CinePair mixes sound, syncs pixels, and overlays camera profiles with full layout control.
